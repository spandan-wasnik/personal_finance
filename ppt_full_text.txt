SLIDE 2:
  THE CHALLENGE
  The Problem
  Silent Overspending
  Users record expenses but may not realize where they're overspending.
  Fast-Moving Categories
  Spending on Dining, Shopping and Travel can increase quickly.
  Too Late to React
  Budget problems are often discovered only after overspending has happened.
  Shrinking Savings
  Current spending can quietly reduce planned savings.
  Blind to What's Next
  Users may not know how current spending affects upcoming or next month's expenses.
  PROBLEM STATEMENT
  How can we identify overspending early and show users how it can affect their future savings?
